# PowerPoint Karaoke

**[▶ Play it here](https://khanna-vijay.github.io/improv-presentation-karaoke/)** — no install, no sign-up, works on a phone.

One person gives a completely confident presentation over a slide deck they have
**never seen before**. They do not know the next image until it appears. The
slides keep advancing. They keep talking.

That is the whole game. The comedy is not in the pictures — it is in watching
somebody explain an absurd image with total institutional conviction.

## How to run a round

1. Press **SPIN**. The wheel lands on a talk format — TED Talk, Shark Tank,
   Board Room, Mission Brief, PhD Thesis, Product Launch, Motivational Keynote
   or Story.
2. Read the performer their **Example Scenario**. Never show them the slides.
3. Press **▶ Start presenting** and hand over. Advance with `→`.

Press **?** in the app for the full guide: why it builds spontaneity, group
variants, a worked sample script and coaching notes.

## What is in here

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The whole app. One file, no build step, no dependencies. |
| `app/library.js` | The slide index — beats, arcs, formats, scenarios. |
| `app/preview/` | Web-weight images. |
| `docs/` | Design notes: the taxonomy, the narrative arcs, the pipeline. |

Every image was generated for this project. The decks are assembled live in the
browser on a narrative arc, so no two spins produce the same talk.
